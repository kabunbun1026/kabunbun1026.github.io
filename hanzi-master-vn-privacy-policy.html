Privacy Policy for Hanzi Master VN

Effective Date: March 5, 2026

Vanung University Research Team ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Hanzi Master VN mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal information.

Hanzi Master VN is designed to run entirely offline for its core features. The App does not require you to create an account, does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal identifiers, and does not upload your learning data, progress, or usage history to any external servers.

1.1 Local Storage

The App stores your learning progress, points, and settings locally on your device using the device's internal storage. If you uninstall the app or clear the app's data, this local progress will be deleted. We do not have access to this data.

2. Permissions

To function properly, the App may request certain device permissions:

  • Network Access: Used strictly for downloading the initial bundle from the app store, and possibly for checking if there's an internet connection if a feature temporarily requires it (such as loading an external email client via Intent). However, the app's core learning modules do not transmit data over the network.
  • Microphone / Speech Recognition (TTS): The App uses your device's built-in Text-To-Speech (TTS) engine to pronounce Chinese characters. This process happens locally on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The App may contain links to other sites (such as opening your email app to send feedback). If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that third party's site or app. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit.

We use Android Capacitor to package our web application. The Capacitor framework may automatically collect standard crash logs or anonymous device info depending on your device's broader Android settings (managed by Google Play Services), but we, the developers, do not track or store this data.

4. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

  • By utilizing the "Feedback" section within the App.
